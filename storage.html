Backup & restore

A backup is a snapshot of everything this site has saved in this browser, taken at the moment you tap the button: your favorites, bookmarks, theme, colors and text size, display and search settings, saved places and every other preference. Save it to a file to keep it safe or to move to another phone or computer, then restore it there.

Saved videos, pictures, PDFs and pages are not part of a backup. They load again when needed.
