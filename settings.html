Stronger accent color

Carries your accent color onto card borders, titles and controls in Research, Gallery and the viewers. Only shows when an accent color is chosen.

Install app

Add Lanthano Research to this device so it opens like an app.

App updates

The site checks for a new version each time you open it. This compares everything your browser has saved (pages, PDFs, pictures and videos) with the live site and replaces whatever changed. Nothing is downloaded to your device, and your favorites, bookmarks and settings are kept.

Checking your version…

The quick check above looks at pages and scripts and takes a moment. Deep check also compares every saved picture, PDF and video with the live site, which can take a while.

Always fill slots

Off: only good matches are shown. On: weaker partial matches fill each section up to its number.

Remember my searches

Shows your recent searches when you tap the empty search box on the home page. They stay on this device.
