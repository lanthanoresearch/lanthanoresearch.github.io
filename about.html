About Lanthano Research

Who Are We?

That is not important, or disclosed.

Our identities remain hidden. The focus is not on personalities, credentials, or public recognition. The focus is on the ideas, evidence, and arguments presented.

What Do We Do?

We investigate, document, and analyze theories, historical claims, biblical topics, unexplained events, and controversial ideas.

What began as private research and the writing of in-depth papers has grown into a public effort to share findings, raise questions, and challenge accepted narratives.

Our goal is not to demand belief, but to encourage examination.

Every claim should be tested. Every assumption should be questioned. Every idea should stand or fall on the strength of its evidence.

Our Purpose

Lanthano exists to explore what others overlook, and to find out truth.

Lanthano Research is a collection of alternative history, conspiracy theories, controversial ideas, unique biblical interpretations and frameworks, and much more. It is not built around the modern consensus. Instead, it focuses on preserving ideas, claims, theories, and evidence that may otherwise become difficult to find or eventually disappear from the internet.

The goal is not necessarily to be the final word on any subject. It is to be a launching point. Rather than forcing someone to spend hours searching through scattered websites to find different theories and their supporting evidence, Lanthano brings them together in one clean, accessible place. Whether someone ultimately accepts, rejects, or simply investigates a theory further, Lanthano gives them a significant head start in their research.
