Preserve the Research

Lanthano Research exists to document, investigate, and preserve information that may otherwise become difficult to find or access.

A website is not permanent. Websites can be taken down, hosting can change, files can disappear, and information can become inaccessible.

The research should not depend entirely on this website remaining online.

One of the simplest ways to help ensure that Lanthano Research continues to exist is to keep an offline copy of it.

Why Keep a Copy?

Downloading the archive gives you a copy of the research that exists independently of Lanthano Research's website.

You can keep the archive on your computer, an external drive, or another storage device.

The purpose is simple: the information should continue to exist even if the website does not.
